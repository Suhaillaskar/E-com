How to book

Booking takes about two minutes. You can pre-book as far as six weeks out.

  1. 1

    Set your dates

    Choose the hub, pick-up day and time, and how long you need the vehicle: 4 to 12 hours, 1 to 28 days, or 1 to 4 weeks.

  2. 2

    Pick what's free

    Green means free for your whole trip. If it is taken, the card shows the next date it opens up, and one tap moves your search there.

  3. 3

    Add extras and pay

    Choose a second helmet, a driver, a child seat or an extra-km pack. Pre-book with an advance now, or pay everything at the hub.

  4. 4

    Show up and ride

    Bring your original driving licence and a photo ID. We inspect the vehicle with you, take the deposit and hand over the keys.

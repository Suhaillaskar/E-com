# 🚗 Dhansiri Wheels – Bike & Car Rental Website

**Dhansiri Wheels** is a bike and self-drive car rental website designed for customers in Guwahati, Assam. It provides a convenient platform to explore available vehicles, compare rental prices, check availability, and book vehicles for short-term or long-term rentals.

## 🌐 Project Overview

The platform offers a user-friendly rental experience where customers can select their preferred pickup location, date, time, and rental duration to find available vehicles.

Users can choose from a variety of bikes, scooters, cars, SUVs, and electric vehicles.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Vehicle Listings:** Browse bikes, scooters, cars, SUVs, and electric vehicles.
- **Availability Checking:** Check vehicle availability based on selected rental dates.
- **Flexible Rentals:** Rent vehicles hourly, daily, or weekly.
- **Multiple Pickup Hubs:** Select from four pickup locations in Guwahati.
- **Online Booking:** Book vehicles according to availability.
- **Advance Payment:** Pre-book vehicles with a 20% advance payment.
- **Rental Pricing:** View hourly, daily, and weekly rental rates.
- **Vehicle Information:** View specifications, seating capacity, transmission, fuel type, and vehicle details.
- **Booking Management:** Access personal bookings through the My Bookings section.
- **Additional Services:** Optional helmets, drivers, child seats, and extra-kilometre packages.
- **Cancellation Policy:** Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. **Select Pickup Details:** Choose your pickup hub, date, and time.
2. **Choose a Vehicle:** Browse the fleet and check available vehicles.
3. **Select Rental Duration:** Choose hourly, daily, or weekly rental options.
4. **Add Extras:** Select additional services if required.
5. **Complete Booking:** Pay the advance amount or choose to pay at the pickup hub.
6. **Pick Up & Ride:** Present your valid driving licence and photo ID to collect the vehicle.

## 💰 Rental Policies

- Bikes: Minimum age of 18 years.
- Cars: Minimum age of 21 years.
- A valid original driving licence is required.
- Government-issued photo ID is required.
- Daily distance limits apply.
- Additional kilometres are charged separately.
- Vehicles must be returned with the original fuel level.
- Electric vehicles must be returned with at least 20% battery charge.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of Dhansiri Wheels is to simplify the bike and self-drive car rental process by providing customers with an easy-to-use platform for vehicle discovery, availability checking, transparent pricing, and convenient bookings.
